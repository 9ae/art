Inspired by “The Ballad of Reading Gaol”, a poem written by Oscar Wilde while in prison. This is a story about a man sentenced to spend eternity as the read-write pointer of a Turning Machine. Marking, erasing, moving along an a winding film strip that has no start nor end.
