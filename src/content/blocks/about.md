I create graphic novel zines—stories that make the familiar feel strange. My work explores isolation, wanting something just out of reach, and living with a blurry memory, unable to tell what is real or imaginary.

I grew up moving between Brooklyn and Queens and attended five elementary schools. When it was hard to make friends, I made up worlds and stories in my head. Those imagined worlds still shape the stories I tell today.
