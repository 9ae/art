Inspired by creatures of Chinese mythology, the nine-tailed fox and the moon rabbit, this triptych tells the story of a forbidden love between two creatures of the different kingdoms.

Media: Granulating watercolors, india ink, fountain pen ink, and stamps (for lettering)

Entry in [Comics in the City 2025 Exhibition](https://www.flushingtownhall.org/show-details/comics-in-the-city-sequential-art-is-artist-talk)
